Price Book

Enter pack contents and price. E.g. Eggs → 6 each · £1.80/pk. Chicken → 500 g · £3.50/pk. Loose p/kg items → 1000 g · £X/pk.

Or bulk-import a price CSV (columns Ingredient, Product, Category, Pack size (qty), Pack unit, Pack price, Store, Aliases). Priced rows update the book; alias-only rows seed matching before a price exists.

Log items from a receipt. Enter pack size (e.g. 6 each), how many packs bought, and price per pack. Price book updates automatically.
